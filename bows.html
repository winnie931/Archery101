Getting started

Types of bows

Every bow has the same goal: send an arrow safely and consistently. These are three common places to begin.

Recurve

A simple, versatile bow used in beginner programs and Olympic competition.

Compound

A modern bow with cams that reduce holding weight at full draw.

Traditional

A classic style with minimal equipment, including longbows and barebows.

Ask a certified instructor to help you choose the proper draw weight and fit.
