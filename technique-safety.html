Non-negotiable

Five rules for every range

Local commands differ, but these habits travel. When in doubt, lower the bow and ask the range officer.

01

Wait for the command

Do not approach the line, nock an arrow, or collect arrows until the range officer says it is safe.

02

Point only downrange

Keep every bow, loaded or unloaded, aimed toward the target and backstop. Never sky-draw.

03

Check the lane

Confirm people, animals, and equipment are clear. Stop immediately if anyone calls “FAST” or “CEASE FIRE.”

04

Inspect before shooting

Check limbs, string, nocks, arrows, and fasteners. Retire cracked arrows and damaged strings.

05

Collect together

Walk, never run, to targets only after the all-clear. Pull arrows one person at a time while others stand aside.

Essential protection

Small gear, meaningful comfort

Arm guard
Keeps loose clothing and the forearm clear of string contact.
Finger tab or glove
Protects the drawing fingers and gives the string a clean surface.
Chest guard
Controls clothing near the string; especially helpful with layers.
Proper footwear
Closed-toe shoes with stable footing are standard range practice.
!
Fit check

Remove jewelry and secure hair or loose clothing. Any item near the string path can change the shot or cause injury.
